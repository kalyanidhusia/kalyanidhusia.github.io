Start by framing the project

Before choosing software, start with the scientific decision the analysis needs to support.

Biological questions

  • What is the primary biological or clinical question?
  • What comparison, phenotype, or outcome matters?
  • What would count as a useful result?
  • Is the goal discovery, validation, prediction, or mechanism?

Data readiness

  • What data already exist, and in what format?
  • How many samples, groups, time points, or replicates?
  • What metadata are available?
  • What is the intended manuscript, grant, software, or preliminary-data output?

Project areas

Genomics & transcriptomics

RNA-seq, WES/WGS, single-cell analysis, differential expression, variant workflows, and reproducible pipelines.

Proteomics & proteogenomics

Quantitative proteomics, pathway analysis, variant-peptide evidence, and multi-omics integration.

AI & machine learning

Predictive modeling, benchmarking, feature analysis, LLM-assisted scientific workflows, and evidence-aware AI tools.

Clinical & multimodal data

SQL-based data integration, clinical/molecular linkage, reproducible analysis, and translational decision-support concepts.

Scientific computing

Nextflow, HPC, containers, workflow scaling, and emerging computational capabilities including quantum chemistry.

Grant & manuscript support

Study design, computational feasibility, preliminary analyses, figures, methods, and reproducibility planning.
