Cardio Beat

The acetylation switch, played as rhythm

Same switchboard, new instrument.

Heart cells tune gene activity with removable acetyl marks on histone tails. Writers (HATs) add marks — chromatin relaxes and the gene can be transcribed. Erasers (HDACs) remove marks — the gene goes quiet. Readers such as BRD4 dock on the marks and recruit RNA polymerase, and the acetyl groups come from acetyl-CoA, wiring metabolism into gene regulation. HDAC inhibitors like SAHA are studied as cardiometabolic therapies — and are double-edged, because blocking the erasers de-represses everything.

♥ Same concept as Chromatin Command and HAT-a-Mole — three games, one switchboard. Here the writer's job is timing: transcription happens on the polymerase's beat, and you acetylate exactly when it matters.

Your mission

  • GREEN lanes = protective genes (SERCA2a, MYH6, PGC-1α). A note is polymerase arriving — tap its lane as the note crosses the line to acetylate in time. Hit = transcript; miss = the gene stayed closed and the heart pays.
  • RED lane = NPPA, a fetal stress gene. Never tap it. A red note that passes untapped is a stress gene kept silent (bonus). Tapping it writes a mark on the stress gene — toxic transcript.
  • SAHA overdrive (charged by your hit streak): for 3 seconds every note auto-hits — greens AND the red lane. Total de-repression: protective program fully on, stress program leaking. Not gene-specific; neither is the drug.
  • 60 notes per run. Your first 2 damaging mistakes are free practice; the run ends if heart health hits zero.

Named simplifications — what the game fakes

  • Four timing lanes stand in for thousands of loci; real HATs are not gene-aware — targeting comes from transcription factors and readers recruiting them, not from a player choosing lanes.
  • Transcription is not a single beat to hit: polymerase initiates, pauses, and elongates over seconds to minutes; the hit window is the teaching version of "the mark must be in place when the machinery arrives."
  • Your hit streak charging SAHA is game logic, not pharmacology — the real drug is dosed, not earned.
  • The true part: writers open and erasers close; a mark must be present for transcription to proceed; stress-program genes are silenced by default and harmful when de-repressed; and HDAC inhibition is indiscriminate — the overdrive that auto-hits the red lane is exactly SAHA's double edge.
