Pharmacokinetics · the therapeutic window

A drug only works inside its window.

After a dose, drug concentration in your blood rises (absorption), then is constantly pulled down as the liver metabolizes and the kidneys excrete it — together called clearance. The therapeutic window is the concentration range between too low to work and high enough to be toxic. Dosing is the art of staying inside it over time.

After playing you can…
  • Explain why drug levels rise and fall: absorption versus clearance (liver and kidneys).
  • Define the therapeutic window: below it, no effect; above it, toxicity.
  • Say why dose timing matters — a missed dose drops you out of the window.
