Codon typeRNADNAFunction
StartAUGATGInitiates translation; codes for methionine (Met)

The three stop codons have classic names: UAA "ochre", UAG "amber", UGA "opal". Wrong amino acid = missense mutation. Hit a wall or yourself = the chain misfolds. First three codons are practice — mistakes are free.
