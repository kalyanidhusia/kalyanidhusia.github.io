AR INBRE · Student Training

Bio Arcade

Six short games. Each one teaches a single biomedical concept — the game mechanic is the biology, not decoration on top of it.

Learn (flashcard) → Play (the mechanic is the concept) → Check (6-question quiz, pass at 5)
Module 1 · Central Dogma I

Polymerase Runner

Transcription: you are RNA polymerase — pair the bases by hydrogen bonds, watch T shatter into U at the transcription zone, and build an mRNA.

~6 min · phone-friendly Play
Module 2 · Central Dogma II

Ribosome Snake

Translation: the ribosome reads codons AUG → stop, tRNAs deliver amino acids, and release factors end the chain at UAA / UAG / UGA.

~6 min · phone-friendly Play
Module 3 · Protein Folding

Fold-Tris

The hydrophobic effect: oily residues bury in the core, polar ones face water — and misfolding leads to aggregation unless chaperones step in.

~6 min · phone-friendly Play
Module 4 · Immunology

Phagocyte Patrol

Phagocytosis & opsonization: you are a macrophage; antibody tags turn hard-to-catch pathogens into easy meals — innate and adaptive immunity as a team.

~6 min · phone-friendly Play
Module 5 · Pharmacology

Drug Flight

Pharmacokinetics: dose timing keeps a drug inside its therapeutic window — too high is toxic, too low does nothing, and the liver and kidneys clear it.

~5 min · phone-friendly Play
Module 6 · Microbiology

Lysis Lab

Antibiotics & resistance: wall-targeting drugs burst bacteria but spare your wall-less cells — until β-lactamase appears, and clavulanate counters it.

~6 min · phone-friendly Play

Educational content, not medical advice. Each game states its simplifications; the mechanics model one concept, not full biological reality.
