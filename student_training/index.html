Student training hub

Learn computational biology by building something real.

A growing training pathway for students who want hands-on experience with biomedical data, coding, reproducible research, multi-omics, and AI-assisted scientific workflows.

Placeholder page · training modules being built

You do not need to know everything before you start.

Students may arrive from biology, biochemistry, computer science, mathematics, or another STEM field. The goal is to build enough shared vocabulary and technical skill to contribute to a real research question.

Biology-first student

Learn Linux, Git, scripting, data structures, statistics, and reproducible analysis while applying your biological knowledge.

Computing-first student

Learn how biomedical experiments are designed, what metadata mean, how biological evidence is evaluated, and why context matters.

Already mixed?

Move quickly into a defined project involving real data, benchmarking, workflow development, visualization, or scientific software.

Starter pathway

01

Research basics

Questions, hypotheses, controls, metadata, and reading a scientific paper.

02

Computing basics

Linux shell, files, Git/GitHub, environments, and reproducibility.

03

Python or R

Data frames, visualization, statistics, and scientific scripting.

04

Biomedical data

RNA-seq, genomics, proteomics, clinical data, and common analysis patterns.

05

Mini-project

Answer one defined question and produce a figure, analysis, workflow, or software contribution.

Possible project tracks

Cancer multi-omics

Connect DNA, RNA, protein, and phenotype to investigate tumor biology and treatment response.

Bioinformatics workflows

Build and test reproducible workflows using Nextflow, containers, Git, and HPC resources.

Machine learning & AI

Benchmark models, evaluate features, test LLM-assisted workflows, and learn how to validate computational claims.

Proteomics

Analyze quantitative protein data, perform pathway analysis, visualize results, and connect them to biological questions.

Scientific software

Turn repeated analysis tasks into reusable scripts, packages, dashboards, or research tools.

Data visualization

Learn to communicate complex biological results clearly through figures, reports, and interactive outputs.
